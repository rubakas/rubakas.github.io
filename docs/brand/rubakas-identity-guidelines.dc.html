Rubakas · Identity guidelines02 · Brand

A small crew with a big crew behind it.

Who we are

A group of friends who spent years building for the web with Ruby on Rails. Today we run crews of AI agents, and build the tooling that keeps them disciplined: hardened specs, clear roles, honest review.

The name

Rub- for Ruby. Рубака (rubaka), from рубить, "to hew": shaping raw output into clean work. We keep the hew-and-carve reading, and leave the blades out.

Rubakas · Identity guidelines06 · Colour

Three colours, one red

The same red is used by day and by night. It sits at the point of equal contrast against both neutrals.

Rubakas redHEX #EE1E1ERGB 238 30 30CMYK 0 87 87 7The mark and signals. About 10%.
PaperHEX #FAFAF8RGB 250 250 248CMYK 0 0 1 2Day background. About 30%.
SootHEX #161616RGB 22 22 22CMYK 0 0 0 91Night background and all ink. About 60%.
Proportion
  • The R reaches about 4.2:1 against red in both modes.
  • Text is soot on paper or paper on soot (about 17:1).
  • Red is never used as a background and never for body text.
  • The CMYK values are a starting point. Check a proof before printing.
