# Surface brief: Rubakas homepage

Scope: the single homepage (`src/routes/+page.svelte`) and the brand assets the guide lists for the web. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: a business lead arriving cold decides in seconds whether this small crew is credible for AI consulting and strategy, or for building agentic-first apps and platforms. A developer arriving from github.com/rubakas is secondary. Action: email contact@rubakas.com through a `mailto:` button; no forms.

Proof and content: plain statements of method from the Identity Guidelines v1.0 (`docs/brand/`), the two confirmed offerings, and the GitHub link. No clients, testimonials, pricing or demos. `agent-notes` and Plugari stay off the page.

Constraints: static SvelteKit 3 on GitHub Pages, Tailwind 4. The guide is the fixed visual world. Self-hosted Montserrat and IBM Plex Mono. Reduced-motion and no-JS visitors get the final mark immediately. Content and the button are visible from first paint; motion never gates them.

Unresolved decisions: none open. Defaults taken where the user did not answer: self-hosted fonts, a red call-to-action tile with the mark's cut (the owner chose it over a paper outline; red stays out of bands, links and text), a night cover with a day body and a night close, copy drafted from the guide's own voice for the user to approve.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the mark assembling itself. A flat red cube tumbles out of the dark, locks into the cut tile, and then gets out of the way. It refuses the category hero (headline, subhead, art on the right) and every terminal or CRT effect from the old site.

OWN-WORLD: Identity Guidelines v1.0 as written. Soot #161616 night bands, paper #FAFAF8 day bands, one red #EE1E1E used only in the mark, in focus signals and in the call-to-action tile. Montserrat 700 headings in sentence case, Plex Mono 400 capitals for the tagline and button. One-pixel soot rules, no cards, no shadows. Neutrals only from the guide's own pages (#3D3D3D, #6A7282, #99A1AF, #E2E8F0). With the words removed it is still a black band, a red hexagon and one tracked wordmark.

STORY: A cold visitor learns in one viewport that Rubakas is a small crew running AI agents on real software work, believes it from plain statements of method rather than claims, and emails. A developer finds github.com/rubakas at the close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 100svh of soot. Centre stage, the stacked lockup: the mark at about min(34vw, 30svh, 220px), the wordmark at 0.414 of the mark's height tracked +0.2em, the tagline "Taste is load-bearing" in Plex Mono capitals at 0.157, one plain sentence beneath, then a red "Talk with us" tile with the mark's cut corners. The cube plays from 0.3 s to 2.7 s and locks with a 4% pulse as the R cuts in. Text and button are visible from the first paint.

FORM: The Lock-in, candidate 1 of 7 on the ordered list, seed 841e06f0.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
